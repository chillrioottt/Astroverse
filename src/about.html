ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Shubhashish Das. I've been fascinated by astronomy for as long as I can remember. The night sky has always inspired me to ask questions about the universe—how stars form, what distant worlds might be like, and how the cosmos has changed over time. That curiosity grew into a lasting passion and encouraged me to learn more about astronomy and the ideas that help us understand the universe. Astroverse is a place to share that enthusiasm and explore the wonders of space together. I hope to make it easier for curious minds to discover astronomy, follow exciting developments, and appreciate the science behind what we see in the sky. Whether you're just beginning to explore or already love learning about the cosmos, I invite you to look around, stay curious, and keep asking questions. There is always more to discover.

Thank you for visiting Astroverse and being part of this journey of discovery!
